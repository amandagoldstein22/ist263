Fit and materials

One size fits most. Every hat is a classic five-panel trucker with a structured front panel, a breathable polyester mesh back, a pre-curved brim, and an adjustable snapback closure. Patches are embroidered and sewn on by hand.

To clean, spot treat with cold water and let it air dry. Please don’t put it in the washing machine or the dryer — it will lose its shape.

Shipping and returns

Free standard shipping on all orders over $75 within the United States. Standard shipping is $5 and takes 3 to 5 business days. Orders placed before 2pm Pacific ship the same day. International rates are calculated at checkout.

If it isn’t right, send it back within 30 days for a full refund, as long as it’s unworn with the tags still on. Email us and we’ll send you a prepaid return label.
