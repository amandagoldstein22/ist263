About Coastal Generation

Born in Hermosa Beach. Inspired by every coast. Made for everywhere.

Where it started

Coastal Generation was born from a love for life by the water and everything that comes with it — the sun, the people, the places, and that feeling of being near the coast. Hermosa Beach, California is home, but the inspiration behind Coastal Generation goes far beyond one place.

It started with a hat. One patch, one beach town, one idea we couldn’t stop thinking about: what if the feeling of being somewhere coastal was something you could actually carry with you?

Every coast

The feeling of being by the water, discovering new places, and spending long days in the sun is something you can find on coastlines all around the world. We take inspiration from everywhere: the laid-back lifestyle of California, the warmth of Panama, the effortless style of Australia, and summers along the Mediterranean.

Every coast has its own look, culture, and personality, and that’s what makes each one special. Coastal Generation brings those influences together while always staying true to where we started.

How we make things

We carefully create and curate pieces that feel timeless, effortless, and easy to make your own. From bikinis and beach towels to hoodies and hats, our collections take inspiration from classic coastal style and give it a younger, fresher, and more playful feel. It’s coastal luxury for a new generation.

Every patch is designed in house, and every hat is made in small batches so we can keep a close eye on quality. We start with one piece, get it right, and build from there. Our first collection is a line of retro patch trucker hats, each one built around a place or a feeling we love.

What being coastal means to us

To us, being coastal isn’t about where you live. It’s about the feeling you take with you. Whether you’re spending the summer by the water, traveling somewhere new, or nowhere near the ocean at all, Coastal Generation is made to bring a piece of the coast with you.

Our mission is simple: to make it feel coastal all year round, wherever you are in the world.

Want to carry us?
